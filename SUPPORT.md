# Support

Before reporting a problem, check the [getting started guide](GETTING_STARTED.md) and the [latest release notes](https://github.com/hey-yjenius/PyBOM/releases/latest).

## Report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/hey-yjenius/PyBOM/issues/new) with:

- PyBOM version and Windows version.
- The steps that led to the problem.
- What you expected and what actually happened.
- The exact error message, if available.
- A small, anonymized example when needed to reproduce an import problem.

Do not upload production workbooks, supplier prices, customer details, credentials or other confidential information. Diagnostic bundles and screenshots can contain filenames, local paths or business data; inspect and redact them before sharing.

The application provides **Help > Open Log Folder** and **Help > Export Diagnostic Bundle** for troubleshooting.

## Planning questions

Include the relevant input quantities, dates and units in an anonymized example. Explain which result you are comparing and how you calculated the expected value.

PyBOM does not replace your organization's approval process for purchasing or production changes.
